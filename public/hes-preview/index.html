WE’RE HERE TO HELP

Your questions,
answered clearly.

Who can learn with HES?

Explore diabetes and nutrition education for individuals and families, and health workshops for community groups. Contact HES to discuss your needs and current availability.

What support does HES offer?

The website introduces diabetes education, nutrition education, monitoring education, and group workshops. Specific program formats, fees, and availability need to be confirmed with HES.

Can I book or enroll on this website?

This is a design preview. You can select program options and save consultation preferences, but no appointment is reserved and no enrollment is submitted. Call 757-384-6250 or email lateef@healthedservices.org to ask about availability.

Is the Newport News pilot open?

Not yet. The pilot is being planned for 30 adults with type 2 diabetes and three months of participant support. Funding, readiness, eligibility, and enrollment details must be confirmed before it begins.

Does health education replace medical care?

No. Education helps you understand information and prepare questions. It does not replace diagnosis, treatment, medication decisions, or individualized medical nutrition therapy from qualified professionals.

Can I make a donation here?

You can review a one-time or monthly donation amount, but this preview does not collect money or create recurring payments. HES will confirm payment arrangements and donation terms before donations open.
