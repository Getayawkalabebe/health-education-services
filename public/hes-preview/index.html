FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What to know
before you begin.

Who are these programs for?

The proposed programs are for people seeking diabetes or nutrition education, family members supporting someone with diabetes, and groups interested in health workshops. Ask HES which options fit your needs.

Are ongoing programs available?

This preview includes single-session, ongoing-program, and workshop options. Enrollment is not open here. Availability, prices, and any subscription terms must be confirmed with HES.

Can I book an appointment on this site?

Not yet. You can choose a topic and save your preferred date and time, but this does not send a request or reserve an appointment. Contact HES to discuss availability.

Can I donate through this preview?

Not yet. You can review a one-time or monthly donation amount, but no money is collected. HES will confirm how donations are used, payment arrangements, and tax status before donations open.

Does health education replace medical care?

No. Education can help you understand information and prepare questions for your care team. It does not replace diagnosis, treatment, medication decisions, or individualized medical nutrition therapy.
