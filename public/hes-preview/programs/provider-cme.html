Provider Continuing Medical Education Workshops

Give healthcare teams a structured opportunity to practice clearer conversations about diabetes, glucose reports and self-management education.

Who it’s for

Clinics, health systems and professional organizations planning education for clinicians or interdisciplinary teams.

What you’ll cover

  • Identify a practice gap and write measurable learning objectives
  • Use cases to practice discussing glucose trends and care-team questions
  • Use teach-back to check how clearly an explanation landed
  • Identify a referral or communication improvement to try after the workshop

How it can work

Agree on audience, clinical content, faculty responsibilities and evaluation with the host. A case-based session can combine a short presentation, group discussion and a practical exercise.

What you can take away

Learning objectives, a draft agenda and an evaluation plan. If CME credit is requested, an accredited provider must review and approve the activity before credit is advertised.
