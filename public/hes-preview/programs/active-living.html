Community support

Active Living Initiatives

Make movement easier to start and repeat in the places people already gather. Build activities around what participants enjoy and what their community can support.

Who it’s for

Neighborhood groups, community centers and organizations serving adults with a range of abilities and activity levels.

What you’ll cover

  • Identify practical barriers such as cost, safety or transportation
  • Explore walking, seated or indoor options as appropriate
  • Choose an achievable activity goal and a backup plan
  • Build a peer check-in or recurring group activity

How it can work

Hosts and HES can design an introductory activity, map accessible options and agree on a follow-up. Plan for seating, weather, rest breaks and clear guidance on who should consult their care team before participating.

What you can take away

A realistic movement plan for participants and a simple delivery checklist for hosts. Participation and feedback can guide improvements.
