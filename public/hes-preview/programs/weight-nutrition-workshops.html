Weight & Nutrition Workshops

Give participants time to practice food skills they can use at their next grocery trip or meal. Workshops can be tailored to the foods people eat, the stores they use and the time they have to cook.

Who it’s for

Community centers, faith groups, employers and organizations hosting adult learning sessions.

What you’ll cover

  • Compare food labels using realistic shopping examples
  • Plan a meal or grocery list within a budget
  • Explore flexible portions and substitutions for familiar meals
  • Choose one practical change and a way to check how it went

How it can work

Plan a demonstration, a hands-on small-group activity and a short take-home exercise. Hosts can discuss venue, language, accessibility, audience size and materials with HES.

What you can take away

Participants can leave with a sample shopping plan and one action to try. Hosts can use a short activity or feedback form to see which skills need more practice.
