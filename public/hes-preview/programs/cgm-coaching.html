Patient support

CGM coaching

Turn a continuous glucose monitor (CGM) report into a conversation you can use. Learn to read trends in context and decide which questions are worth taking to your care team.

Who it’s for

Adults using a CGM or considering one, and support people invited by the person using it.

What you’ll cover

  • Get familiar with trend arrows, alerts and time in range
  • Compare patterns across several days alongside meals, movement or schedule changes
  • Practice summarizing one recurring pattern and sharing a report if you choose
  • Identify device questions to check against manufacturer instructions or discuss with your care team

How it can work

Walk through a sample report or review a report you choose to share during a session. Start with one question, such as why a pattern seems to repeat after a particular meal, and note what else was happening at the time.

What you can take away

A plain-language summary of one pattern and questions to discuss at your next visit. Ask separately about device availability, costs and compatibility.
