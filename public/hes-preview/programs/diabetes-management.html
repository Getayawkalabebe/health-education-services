Patient support

Diabetes management

Make diabetes care easier to navigate between appointments. Start with the part of your routine that feels hardest, from understanding a glucose report to preparing for a change in schedule.

Who it’s for

Adults living with diabetes who want practical education after diagnosis or when their routines or care plans change.

What you’ll cover

  • Review the questions you have about your care plan
  • Practice spotting patterns in readings and writing down useful context
  • Build a routine for meals, activity or monitoring that fits your day
  • Prepare questions about targets, symptoms or medicines for your care team

How it can work

In an individual education conversation, bring a typical day and one challenge you want to work on. Together, you can map the challenge, choose a manageable action and discuss what you would review at a follow-up.

What you can take away

A personal learning goal, an everyday action to try and a short list of questions for your next care visit. Your clinical team sets treatment and medication plans.
