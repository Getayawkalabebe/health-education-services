Caregiver and family support

Support someone living with diabetes while respecting their choices. Learn how to ask what help they want, talk about routines without blame and share responsibilities in ways that work for everyone.

Who it’s for

Family members, friends and caregivers, with the person living with diabetes invited to guide the conversation when they wish.

What you’ll cover

  • Ask what support is welcome and when
  • Agree on practical roles for shopping, appointments or reminders
  • Discuss privacy and consent before sharing glucose information
  • Prepare questions about the care team’s action plan and whom to call

How it can work

A family education session can use a common scenario, such as sharing meals or preparing for a trip. Practice a conversation and agree on what each person will handle.

What you can take away

A shared support plan with boundaries, agreed roles and care-team questions.
