Youth & Family Health Literacy Workshop

Help young people and their families understand everyday health messages, ask questions and practice making informed choices together.

Who it’s for

Schools, youth organizations and family groups seeking age-appropriate activities with caregiver involvement.

What you’ll cover

  • Practice reading a food label or comparing drink options
  • Tell the difference between a health claim and its source
  • Practice asking a trusted adult or clinician a health question
  • Choose a family activity or meal-planning habit to try together

How it can work

Use short scenarios, demonstrations and a family take-home activity. Agree with hosts on age range, accessible materials and caregiver consent or participation requirements.

What you can take away

A practical family activity and a simple way for participants to explain what they learned. Activities focus on skills and avoid public weigh-ins or body comparisons.
